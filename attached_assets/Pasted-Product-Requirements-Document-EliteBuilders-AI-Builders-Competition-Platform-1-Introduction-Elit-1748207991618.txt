Product Requirements Document: EliteBuilders - AI Builders Competition Platform
1. Introduction
EliteBuilders is a web-based competitive arena designed to bridge the gap between AI talent and industry demand. Current hiring practices often rely on algorithmic challenges that don't fully assess a candidate's ability to develop and deploy real-world, product-ready AI solutions. This platform will enable AI-centric companies and non-tech enterprises to identify top-tier talent by evaluating their end-to-end applied-AI product thinking. For AI builders, particularly those who are self-taught or have non-traditional backgrounds, EliteBuilders offers a unique opportunity to showcase their skills through tangible projects, gain recognition, and connect with potential employers or clients. Participants will rapidly craft and submit AI-powered Minimum Viable Products (MVPs)—complete with a prototype, pitch deck, and demo video—in response to company-authored or sponsored challenges. A hybrid scoring system, combining automated LLM-driven evaluations and human judge reviews, will surface top talent on dynamic leaderboards, fostering a competitive and rewarding environment.
2. Goals
 * For Companies/Sponsors:
   * Identify and recruit top AI talent with proven product-building capabilities.
   * Reduce hiring cycle times and improve the quality of hires.
   * Gain access to innovative AI solutions for real-world business problems.
   * Enhance employer branding within the AI community.
 * For AI Builders/Participants:
   * Provide a platform to showcase practical, end-to-end AI project development skills.
   * Gain visibility and connect with potential employers and clients.
   * Earn industry-recognized badges, prizes, and build a strong portfolio.
   * Receive constructive feedback on their AI solutions and product thinking.
 * For the Platform (EliteBuilders):
   * Become the leading platform for applied AI talent assessment and discovery.
   * Foster a vibrant community of AI builders and innovative companies.
   * Generate revenue through company sponsorships and premium features.
3. Target Users
 * AI Builders/Participants:
   * Software developers, data scientists, machine learning engineers, AI enthusiasts (students, professionals, career changers).
   * Individuals (initially solo, potentially teams in the future) seeking to demonstrate practical AI skills.
   * Self-taught learners and those from non-traditional educational backgrounds.
   * Freelancers looking for project opportunities.
 * Companies/Sponsors (Hiring Partners):
   * Tech companies (AI-focused or with AI departments).
   * Non-tech enterprises looking to integrate AI solutions.
   * Recruitment agencies specializing in tech talent.
   * Venture capitalists and incubators looking for promising AI startups/talent.
 * Judges/Reviewers:
   * Experienced AI professionals, product managers, and industry experts.
   * Representatives from sponsoring companies.
4. User Stories
 * As an AI Builder, I want to:
   * Easily browse and filter available challenges to find ones that match my skills and interests.
   * Clearly understand the requirements, evaluation criteria, deadlines, and potential prizes for each challenge.
   * Quickly sign up and create a profile, linking my existing professional accounts (GitHub, portfolio, CV).
   * Receive suggestions for suitable challenges based on my profile.
   * Securely submit my project deliverables (repository link, pitch deck, demo video).
   * Track the status of my submission and receive timely notifications about evaluations.
   * Understand how my submission was scored, including feedback from automated systems and human judges.
   * See my ranking on real-time leaderboards for specific challenges and an overall career score.
   * Automatically receive badges for my achievements (e.g., Top 10%, Category Winner).
   * Be contacted by hiring partners interested in my profile and submissions.
 * As a Company/Sponsor, I want to:
   * Create and publish challenges that address our specific business needs or research areas.
   * Define clear problem statements, provide necessary data packs, and set evaluation rubrics.
   * Manage my active challenges and view participant submissions.
   * Fund prizes to attract top talent to my challenges.
   * Access a ranked list of submissions with comprehensive candidate packets (profile, submission, scores).
   * Directly contact high-potential candidates through the platform.
   * Gain insights into emerging AI talent and innovative solutions.
 * As a Platform Administrator, I want to:
   * Manage user accounts and support requests.
   * Oversee the challenge creation and submission process.
   * Monitor the automated scoring system and coordinate human judge reviews.
   * Ensure the integrity and fairness of the competitions and leaderboards.
   * Manage content on the platform, including challenges, user profiles, and company pages.
   * Track platform usage and key performance metrics.

11. Assumptions
 * Companies are actively seeking better ways to identify applied AI talent beyond traditional methods.
 * AI builders are eager for platforms to showcase their end-to-end project skills.
 * LLMs can provide a meaningful and scalable first-pass evaluation of certain aspects of AI projects (like documentation and pitch decks).
 * Human judges are willing to participate in reviewing top submissions.
 * Sufficiently interesting and well-defined challenges can be sourced from companies or created by the platform.
12. Risks
 * Challenge Quality & Quantity: Difficulty in attracting enough high-quality, engaging challenges from companies.
 * Evaluation Subjectivity/Bias: Ensuring fairness and consistency in both LLM and human evaluations. LLMs can inherit biases, and human judgment can be subjective.
 * Intellectual Property Concerns: Participants may be hesitant to submit innovative ideas without clear IP protection guidelines.
 * User Adoption: Attracting a critical mass of both builders and hiring companies.
 * Scalability of Human Review: Managing the workload for human judges as the platform grows.
 * Technical Complexity: Building and maintaining a robust platform with LLM integration and sandboxed testing environments.
 * Cheating/Plagiarism: Ensuring the authenticity of submissions.

Product Requirements Document: EliteBuilders - AI Builders Competition Platform
1. Introduction
EliteBuilders is a web-based competitive arena designed to bridge the critical gap between AI talent and industry demand for practical, product-ready skills. Traditional technical assessments often focus on algorithmic knowledge, failing to capture a candidate's ability to conceptualize, develop, and present holistic AI solutions. This platform addresses the challenge faced by AI-centric companies—and increasingly, non-tech enterprises—in verifying if candidates can truly "ship" AI products. Simultaneously, it empowers self-taught builders and those with non-traditional backgrounds to showcase tangible AI projects, gain recognition, and connect directly with hiring opportunities.
EliteBuilders will host company-authored or sponsored challenges where solo participants rapidly develop and submit AI-powered Minimum Viable Products (MVPs). Submissions will include not just code, but a functional prototype, a compelling pitch deck, and a demonstration video. A pioneering hybrid scoring system, combining automated LLM-driven evaluations for speed and scale with expert human judge reviews for depth and nuance, will identify top performers. Ranked leaderboards, skill-based badges, and valuable prizes will incentivize participation, while hiring partners gain privileged access to a curated pool of proven AI product builders, shortening hiring cycles and improving talent-job fit.
